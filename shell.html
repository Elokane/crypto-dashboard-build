Value accrual & dilution notes

Discipline: separate hype/narrative from business fundamentals; a token qualifies only if its value appreciates in direct relationship to the business (fees, burns, buybacks, staking revenue). No memecoins. ETH in scope; BTC tracked separately.
Reading MC/FDV: low ratio = large future dilution (HYPE ~0.26 and LIT ~0.25 — the deepest overhangs here); ~1.0 = fully diluted (ETH 1.00; AAVE ~0.95).
Price policy: live trackers only — CoinGecko, then Binance, then Hyperliquid; when none is reachable the last verified prices are shown as a dated snapshot. Fee figures are DefiLlama's 30-day run-rate, annualized ×12.17 — every figure passes automated integrity checks before it is baked; anomalous feeds are quarantined and logged in the open.
Business grade (trial): a measured letter (A–E) from six pass/fail checks on public revenue data — market position, share trend, subsidy-free economics, scale, market growth, independence. Shown alongside the judged scores during a several-week trial; it does not yet drive rankings.
Legend: T1 = on-chain / independently verified · T2 = project-official · T3 = named analyst/press · est = estimate · net-dilutive = new supply currently exceeds the value paid to holders.
